1 Connecting to Your Node

  1. Power on your MeshCore LoRa node and ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone.
  2. Open Meshi. The Scan screen appears automatically.
  3. Tap Scan to discover nearby BLE devices.
  4. Tap your node in the list.
  5. The app connects and navigates to the main screen.
Meshi saves the last connected address and reconnects automatically on the next launch — you may not need to scan at all.

To forget the saved device: Settings → Device → Clear saved device.

2 Sending & Receiving Messages

Public channel:

  1. Open the Channel tab (speech bubble icon in the bottom bar).
  2. Type your message in the input field at the bottom.
  3. Tap the send arrow.

Private channel:

  1. Open the Channels tab (# icon).
  2. Tap the channel you want.
  3. Type and send as above.
Messages are limited to 160 characters. A counter appears as you approach the limit. If you navigate away without sending, your draft is automatically saved.

3 Replying to a Message

  1. In any chat screen, swipe a message bubble to the right.
  2. A quoted excerpt appears above the input field.
  3. Type your reply and tap send.

To cancel the quote without replying, tap the × button on the reply bar.

4 Reacting to a Message

  1. In any chat screen, long-press a received message bubble.
  2. An emoji picker appears: 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 👎
  3. Tap an emoji. Your reaction is sent to the message author over the mesh.
  4. The emoji appears as a small pill chip beneath the bubble.
You can only react to messages sent by others — not your own messages.

5 Searching Messages

  1. In any chat screen, tap the magnifying glass icon in the top bar.
  2. Type your search query. Results filter in real time.
  3. Tap the back arrow to exit search.

6 Exporting Chat History

  1. In any chat screen, tap the share icon in the top bar.
  2. The system share sheet opens with the full chat as plain text.
  3. Choose an app to save or forward the export.

7 Direct Messaging a Contact

  1. Open the Nodes tab (people icon).
  2. Find the contact you want to message.
  3. Tap Message on their contact card.
  4. The DM screen opens. Type and send as usual.

8 Managing Contacts

Save a contact

In the Nodes tab, tap Save on a contact card. The contact persists across sessions.

Set a nickname

  1. Tap the pencil icon on a contact card.
  2. Enter a display name and tap Save.

Assign a tag

  1. Tap the tag icon on a contact card.
  2. Enter a short tag (e.g. "Base Camp") and tap Save.

Tags appear as filter chips at the top of the Nodes list.

Mark as verified

  1. Tap the shield icon on a contact card.
  2. Confirm to mark the contact as verified.
Use verified status after confirming a contact's identity in person, e.g. by comparing public key fingerprints.

9 Creating & Joining Private Channels

Create a new channel

  1. Open the Channels tab.
  2. Tap New Channel (or the + button).
  3. Enter a channel name. A random 128-bit AES key is generated automatically.
  4. Tap Create.

Join via QR code

See Section 10.

10 Sharing a Channel via QR Code

To share:

  1. Open the Channels tab.
  2. Tap the QR code icon on the channel you want to share.
  3. A QR code is displayed. Have the other person scan it.

To join from a QR code:

  1. Scan the QR code with any standard QR scanner.
  2. The scanner opens the Meshi app automatically.
  3. A dialog appears: "Join Channel? Join private channel '…' shared via QR code?"
  4. Tap Join. The channel is added to a free slot.

11 Advertising Your Presence

  1. In the Channel tab, tap the tower icon (📡) in the top bar.
  2. If location permission is granted, your current GPS coordinates are included.
  3. If not yet granted, approve the permission request to include coordinates.
For ongoing location inclusion in every advertisement, use persistent location sharing instead (see Section 12).

12 Enabling Location Sharing

  1. In the Channel tab, tap the location pin icon in the top bar.
  2. Grant location permission if prompted.
  3. The icon turns blue — all future advertisements include your GPS coordinates.
  4. Tap the icon again to turn location sharing off.

Your position will appear as a marker on other users' Map tabs.

13 Activating SOS Beacon

  1. In the Channel tab, tap the SOS button in the top bar.
  2. The button turns red — a flood advertisement is sent every 30 seconds.
  3. Tap the SOS button again to stop the beacon.
SOS mode respects Stealth mode. If Stealth is active, SOS advertisements are suppressed.

14 Enabling Stealth Mode

  1. In the Channel tab, tap the eye icon in the top bar.
  2. The icon changes to a crossed eye and turns teal — stealth is active.
  3. You can still receive messages but will not be detected by other nodes.
  4. Tap again to resume normal advertising.

15 Blocking a Contact

  1. Open the Nodes tab.
  2. Tap the block icon (🚫) on the contact's card.
  3. Confirm in the dialog.

The contact's card turns red. All future messages from them are silently ignored and never saved to the database.

16 Unblocking a Contact

From the Nodes tab: Tap the block icon on the red-tinted contact card to unblock.

From Settings:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Scroll to Blocked Contacts.
  3. Tap Unblock next to the contact ID.

17 Backing Up Contacts

  1. Open Settings (gear icon).
  2. Scroll to Device → Backup contacts and tap it.
  3. The system share sheet opens with a JSON file containing all nicknames, tags, and verified flags.
  4. Save to Files, email to yourself, or copy to another device.

18 Restoring Contacts from Backup

  1. Ensure the backup JSON file is accessible on the device (Downloads, Files, etc.).
  2. Open Settings → Device → Restore contacts.
  3. Select the backup JSON file in the file picker.
  4. A dialog confirms the number of contacts imported.
Restoring merges into existing data — it does not delete contacts already on the device. Existing entries are overwritten by the backup values.

19 Pinging a Contact

From the Nodes tab:

  1. Find the contact in the list.
  2. Tap the Ping button on their card.
  3. The RTT appears in a snackbar (e.g. "Ping Alice: ~340ms RTT").

From a DM conversation:

  1. Tap the ping icon in the DM top bar.
  2. The RTT appears in a snackbar.

20 Viewing the Channel Utilisation Graph

  1. Open any channel chat screen (public or private).
  2. Tap the bar chart icon in the top bar.
  3. A bottom sheet opens showing messages per hour over the last 24 hours.
  4. Tap outside the sheet or swipe it down to close.

21 Viewing Node & Radio Stats

  1. Open the Channel tab (public channel).
  2. Tap the info icon (ℹ) in the top bar.
  3. A dialog shows: node name, firmware version, hardware model, battery %, free storage, and radio configuration.
  4. Tap Close to dismiss.

22 Viewing the SNR Signal Trend

What is it?

The three-bar signal icon in a DM conversation top bar is the SNR trend graph toggle. SNR stands for Signal-to-Noise Ratio, measured in decibels (dB). It reflects how cleanly your node is receiving radio packets from that contact through the mesh.

A higher SNR means a stronger, cleaner signal. A lower or negative SNR means the signal is weak or noisy.

Tapping the icon reveals a sparkline chart showing the SNR value recorded for each recent incoming message — up to the last 30 readings.

Why is it useful?

  • Helps you judge whether you and your contact are within reliable mesh range
  • A downward trend may mean you are moving apart or conditions are changing — a useful cue to relay through another node or reposition
  • Useful for comparing different locations or antenna setups

How to use it

Available once at least two messages have been received from the contact.

  1. Open a DM conversation.
  2. Tap the signal bars icon in the top bar (only visible when enough readings exist).
  3. A sparkline chart appears at the top of the message list showing received SNR (dB) over recent messages.
  4. Tap the icon again to hide the chart.
The data comes from SNR values embedded in each incoming radio packet by the MeshCore node. Up to 30 readings per contact are stored locally in the app's database.

23 Replying from a Notification

  1. A message notification appears in the status bar.
  2. Expand the notification (swipe down or long-press it).
  3. Tap Reply.
  4. Type your reply in the inline text field.
  5. Tap the send arrow.

The reply is sent over the mesh and the notification is automatically dismissed. You do not need to open the app.

24 Playing Games

Challenging a contact

  1. Open the Nodes tab.
  2. Find the contact you want to play.
  3. Tap the game button on their card: Reversi, Chess, TTT, or BSH.
  4. An invite is sent. The game screen opens when they accept.

Battleship placement phase

  1. Select a ship from the chip bar.
  2. Toggle H / V to choose orientation.
  3. Tap a cell on the grid to place the ship.
  4. Tap Undo to remove the last placed ship.
  5. Once all 5 ships are placed, tap Ready.
The screen always clearly shows whose turn it is. Valid moves are highlighted in Chess. In Battleship, your fleet and the enemy waters are shown on separate grids.

25 Viewing Game History

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Game History under the Debug section.
  3. A leaderboard shows wins, losses, and draws for all four games.

26 Performing a Panic Wipe

This is irreversible. All messages, contacts, nicknames, game records, and settings will be permanently deleted.
  1. Open Settings.
  2. Scroll to the Danger Zone section at the bottom.
  3. Tap Panic Wipe.
  4. Read the confirmation dialog carefully.
  5. Tap Wipe Everything.

The BLE connection is closed and the app returns to the scan screen.

27 Changing the App Theme

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Appearance, tap one of the three chips: Dark, Light, or System.

The theme changes immediately.

28 Configuring Notifications

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Notifications, toggle Direct message notifications and/or Channel message notifications independently.

29 Configuring the Battery Alert

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Battery Alert, toggle Low battery alert on.
  3. Use the slider to set the alert threshold (5%–50%, default 20%).
The service polls battery level every 5 minutes. The alert fires once per charge cycle and resets automatically when the battery recovers above the threshold.

30 Viewing the Crash Log

  1. Open Settings → Debug → Crash Log.
  2. The most recent unhandled exception stack trace is shown.
  3. Use the Copy button to copy the trace to the clipboard.

If no crash has occurred, the log is empty.
